        FAQ
        
Good to know

      

      

        
Do I need to bring my own wig?
Yes — bring your wig or unit and we'll install and style it for you.

        
What's the difference between Basic Installation and Installation With Style?
Basic Installation (R150) secures and blends your wig. Installation With Style (R200) adds styling such as parting, laid edges and baby hairs.

        
Can I book wig care services on their own?
Absolutely. Wig Wash (R70), Lace Cutting (R50) and Glue Removal (R50) can be booked separately or combined.

        
How do I confirm my booking?
Send your request through the booking form or WhatsApp. We'll reply to confirm your date and time.
